1. 처리하는 개인정보

항목 내용

제보 정보 (오싹알람) 이용자가 보낸 제보 내용, 콘텐츠 사용 동의 여부, 보낸 계정 이름

2. 처리 목적

  • 게시물 댓글에 안내된 키워드를 남긴 이용자에게, 이용자가 요청한 자료(정부·공공기관 공식 안내 링크 모음)를 인스타그램 메시지로 보내기 위해
  • 같은 이용자에게 같은 자료를 중복으로 보내지 않기 위해
  • 댓글·문의 메시지에 답하기 위해
  • 오싹알람: 이용자가 사용에 동의한 제보만 콘텐츠 소재로 검토하기 위해 (제보자 이름·계정은 콘텐츠에 공개하지 않습니다)

회사는 위 정보를 광고 타기팅, 판매, 프로필 분석에 사용하지 않습니다.

Privacy Policy (English)

98 Company (98컴퍼니) · Effective October 27, 2026

98 Company ("we") operates the accounts @2kids_note (Instagram, Threads), @jupjup_alarm (Instagram, Threads), and @ossak_alarm (Instagram, Threads, YouTube), and the Meta app 줍줍알림 that manages these accounts. This policy explains what personal information we process and why.

1. Information we process

We only process the following when you comment on our posts or send a message to our accounts. We do not collect phone numbers, email addresses, government IDs, or payment information.

  • Comment data: Instagram, Threads, or YouTube username (channel name), app-scoped user ID, comment text, and timestamp
  • Message data: the content and time of messages exchanged with our account
  • Follow status: whether you follow our account (yes/no)
  • Story tips (@ossak_alarm): the tip you send, whether you consent to its use, and the account name it came from

2. Why we process it

  • To send you the material you requested (a list of official government and public-agency links) by Instagram message, after you comment the keyword shown in our post
  • To avoid sending the same material to the same person twice
  • To reply to your comments and inquiries
  • @ossak_alarm: to review tips you agreed to share as content ideas (we never publish the tipster's name or account)

We do not use this information for ad targeting, profiling, or sale.

3. Retention and deletion

  • Comment and message processing records are kept for 90 days and then deleted.
  • Tips are kept for up to 1 year after review; tips without consent are deleted right after review.
  • If you ask us to delete your data, we delete it within 10 business days.

4. Service providers and sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. We use these providers to operate the service:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc.: reading comments and sending replies and messages via the Instagram API and Threads API
  • Google LLC: storing processing records (Google Sheets, Google Apps Script) and reading YouTube comments

5. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a stop to the processing of your information at any time. Unfollowing or blocking our account stops further messages.

6. How to request data deletion

  1. Send the message "정보 삭제" (or "delete my data") to @2kids_note, @jupjup_alarm, or @ossak_alarm on Instagram, or email angeltnqls1004@gmail.com with your Instagram, Threads, or YouTube username.
  2. We delete all comment, message, and tip records about you within 10 business days and confirm by message or email.

7. Children

Our accounts provide parenting, lifestyle, and real-estate information and entertainment content for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 14, and we delete it promptly if we find any.
